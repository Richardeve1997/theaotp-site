01 · The round in numbers

Fifty million to two and a half billion.

Four rounds in about four months, and the last two came three weeks apart.

RoundSizeValuationLed by
Seedundisclosedabout $50MConviction and Greenoaks
Series A$75Mabout $500MKleiner Perkins, early August
Series B$250M$2.5BIndex Ventures and Benchmark, 26 August
Total raised$350M

Founder: Noah Shinn, 23. Sole founder, so far as anyone has reported. He left Sierra in 2025, where he had worked on agents, and is the author of the Reflexion and tau-bench papers. The company is registered as Spear Street Technology. The product has been in private beta since February and is still invite only.

Sources for every number in this table are in sections 02 and 04: the founder's WSJ interview, TechCrunch, Forbes and Quartz.
02 · The founder

In his own words. All of them.

Noah Shinn has posted about Instinct twice. Those two posts carry more signal than the whole press cycle.

  • Noah Shinn on X26 Aug 2026
    I'm Noah, the founder of Instinct.

    The launch post. A personal agent you text or call. No new interfaces. It was trained to use a phone and a computer the way a person does, and it is invite only while they bring up compute. Everything else on this page is a reaction to these few paragraphs.

  • Noah Shinn on X28 Aug 2026
    Instinct + Stripe

    Two days after the round. Purchasing users spend over $1,300 a month through Instinct on average. That is the one number the company has put out about usage.

  • Wall Street Journal, interview26 Aug 2026
    The Latest Viral AI Assistant Rocketing Across Silicon Valley

    The only interview. Shinn disclosed the round to Kate Clark here, called the product a one-stop shop to do almost everything, and said some earlier actions have been switched off. Paywalled.

  • instinct.co
    The company site

    One page. It says what the agent can read (email, messages, screen, audio, location) and that access is limited to a private group while they scale compute. There is no blog and no company X account. The waitlist is behind the login link.

  • noahshinn.com
    Noah Shinn's personal site

    Research bio. Reflexion and tau-bench, the two agent papers he is known for. It does not mention Instinct at all.

  • GitHub
    github.com/noahshinn

    The repos behind those papers, plus two browser-agent projects. Useful if you want to see what he was building before the company existed.

03 · Investors and reputable people

Silicon Valley reviewed this in public, for two weeks.

Investors, founders and operators used the product and wrote about it on X. The praise and the complaints often came from the same people.

The money and the believers. Seed investors, the Stripe founders, and the operators who got in early.

  • Pranav Reddy, Conviction (seed investor)26 Aug
    Instinct is a personal agent that works.

    The seed investor's thesis in public: lots of personal agents exist, this one works because it can do anything a person with a phone and a browser can do.

  • Isabella Garcia-Camargo, Conviction26 Aug

  • Sheel Mohnot, Better Tomorrow Ventures20 Aug
