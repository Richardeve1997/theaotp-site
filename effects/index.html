01Start here

Twenty effects, one set of ingredients.

Every effect in the Reel comes from the same three ingredients: a cut-out of you, an empty shot of the room, and a short script per effect.

I built these in Claude Code from my content system, with no editing app and no timeline. Below, each effect has a looping preview and the prompt I'd give Claude Code to build it. Run the setup prompt once, then add effects one at a time.

The setup prompt (run this first)

I have a vertical talking-head video, talk.mp4 (1080x1920, 60 fps), filmed on a tripod. Set up a small Python project that adds effects to it with ffmpeg, numpy, OpenCV and Pillow.

First build the two things every effect will use:
1. A cut-out of me on every frame: an alpha matte made with Robust Video Matting (mobilenetv3), saved as matte.mp4 in greyscale.
2. A clean plate of the empty room: average the frames where the matte says the background is visible, then fill any area I never move out of.

Then write one compositor that reads the video, the matte and the plate frame by frame, with one effect function per time range, and pipes the frames back to ffmpeg with my original audio. Before rendering the whole video, show me one PNG frame of each effect so I can check it.

You need Claude Code, ffmpeg and Python on your machine, and a video filmed on a tripod so the room doesn't move. The prompts are written for that setup.

02On top of you

Graphics on top.

The simplest ones: something drawn over the footage, timed to the word. No cut-out needed.

1

Live cursor

A design-tool selection box drags open around you, with a size readout and a cursor tagged Claude.

For the first 1.5 seconds, draw a selection box that drags open from my top-left to my bottom-right over 0.6 seconds: a 4px blue outline, white square handles at the corners and edges once it's open, a live "width x height" readout pill under it, and a mouse cursor with a "Claude" name tag riding the moving corner.
2

Chat takeover

A chat window takes over the screen and your own clip arrives as the reply.

Replace the frame with a light grid canvas and a chat window card. Type "Edit this video and add these effects" into a user bubble over 0.4 seconds, show three typing dots, then pop in the reply: my own video playing in a rounded 9:16 tile.
7

Headline storm

Breaking-news ticker bars fly across.

Fly three news ticker bars in from the right, 0.16 seconds apart, each tilted a few degrees: a blue label chip (BREAKING, JUST IN, LIVE) and a white bar with bold text scrolling left. Keep them below my chin.
+

Comment drop

The comment box from the middle of the Reel: it types the keyword and posts it.

Slide an Instagram comment box up from the bottom with my profile picture in it. Type "EDIT" into it, tap the blue send button with a ripple, then drop the posted comment in at the top of the screen.
5

Snap zoom

A hard punch-in on the word.

On the first word, snap the camera in to 1.3x on my face in 0.07 seconds and hold. Crop from the original 4K file so it stays sharp. Never upscale the 1080 render.
03Behind and in front

Graphics that go behind you.

The cut-out is what makes these look expensive. Draw the graphic first, then put you back on top, and it sits on the wall behind your head.

3

Orbit ring

Five tool logos circle your head, passing behind you and in front.

Find the centre of my head from the matte. Orbit five round logo badges on a flat ellipse around it. Draw the far half of the ring and the badges on that side behind me (under the cut-out), and the near half in front.
8

Post wall

A wall of your own Reel covers fills in behind you.

Fill the wall behind me with a 5 by 4 grid of my own Reel covers (a frame from each of my videos), popping in one at a time 0.03 seconds apart and drifting up slowly. Darken the room a little and keep me in front with the matte.
11

Sketch words

A word draws itself by hand on the wall behind your head.

Write "sketch words" on the wall behind me in a handwriting font, revealed left to right over 0.6 seconds as if it's being drawn, with a blue underline. Keep my head in front of it with the matte.
12

Growth curve

A chart climbs up the wall behind you.

Draw a chart on the wall behind me: the axes first, then a blue upward curve with a light fill that draws itself left to right over 0.8 seconds, with a dot riding the tip. Keep me in front.
18

Magazine cover

You become the cover star, with the masthead behind your head.

Make me a magazine cover star: swap the room for a flat backdrop, set a huge masthead behind my head, then put the cover lines and a barcode in front of me.
10

Layer explode

The frame splits into its layers and swings round in 3D.

Split the frame into five layers (the empty room, me, a big type layer, the side list, a waveform) and pull them apart in 3D over 0.55 seconds: turn the stack about 34 degrees and space the layers out along the depth with perspective. Label each layer.
04Cut you out

You, lifted off the room.

These move or copy the cut-out of you over a new background or over the empty room.

4

Clone row

Two more of you slide out either side, all talking in sync.

Over the clean plate, add two copies of me at 66% size sliding out to the left and right with a small overshoot, heads level with mine, all on the same frame. Draw them behind the full-size me.
6

Frame escape

The shot shrinks into a tilted photo and your head breaks out of the frame.

Shrink the shot into a white polaroid tilted -4 degrees on the grid canvas in 0.25 seconds. Crop it so my head sits above the photo's top edge, and draw my cut-out over the border there so my head pokes out of the picture.
13

Paper cutout

You become a paper sticker on a colour collage.

Turn me into a paper sticker: a thick white border around my matte and a soft drop shadow, on a flat collage of a blue circle, a black star and rows of dots. Jitter my angle and position 12 times a second like stop motion.
14

Green screen

The room swaps out for new backgrounds.

Replace the room behind me with flat green for half a second, then cut to two different sky photos.
16

Phone mockup

The Reel shrinks into a phone in the Reels feed.

Shrink the video into a black phone on the grid canvas in 0.28 seconds, rounding the corners as it goes, and put the Reels UI on its screen: my username, a Follow button and the like, comment and share icons.
19

Dolly zoom

The room stretches away while you stay the same size.

Keep me the same size and push the camera into the empty room behind me, from 1.0x to 1.34x across the whole clip, with a light vignette. Use the clean plate as the background so the zoom never shows a ghost of me.
20

Blow away

You break into dust and blow off the screen.

Halfway through my last word, freeze my cut-out and turn it into particles that peel off from right to left and blow away on a wind curve, fading out over 0.75 seconds to show the empty room.
05Redraw you

You, redrawn.

These change your pixels themselves: characters, engraved lines, comic ink.

9

Code body

You get redrawn as live code.

Redraw me as live code. Split the frame into 12x20 pixel cells, pick a monospace character for each cell by its brightness, colour the characters blue to white where the matte says it's me, and scroll faint code behind. Re-pick a few characters every 3 frames so it flickers.
15

Banknote

Your face, engraved on a banknote.

Turn the frame into a banknote: cream paper, a double green border, corner numerals, and my face in a centre oval as a line engraving, where each horizontal line gets thicker as the picture gets darker.
17

Impact frame

One frozen frame becomes a comic-book ink panel.

Freeze one frame and turn it into a black and white comic ink panel: edge lines plus solid shadows, speed lines bursting out from my head, a big IMPACT title, a white flash on the first frames and a quick camera shake.

07What to do now

Pick one. Put it in your next video.

You don't need all twenty. One good effect on the word that matters does more than ten on every line.

  • Never tried this: start with Snap zoom and Live cursor. They need no cut-out.
  • Want it to look expensive: make the cut-out, then try Sketch words or Orbit ring.
  • Want the one people comment on: make the empty room too, then Clone row or Blow away.

See what's possible. Then build it.
