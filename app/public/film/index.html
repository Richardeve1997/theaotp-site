01Start here

One free skill. 66 film styles.

Lemo-Opuscar is a free Claude skill from LemoLab. You pick a style, tell Claude what the film is about, and it writes every frame in code.

No video model and no stock footage. Each style is a written style guide plus a demo film Claude made with it, so you can see exactly what you'll get before you spend the time. The stills on this page come from those demo films. I didn't make the skill and I earn nothing from it.

1

How a film gets made.

Claude reads the style's guide, plans the story, writes the film as code with its own sound and music, then renders it to an MP4. A film takes about 30 to 60 minutes and a fair amount of tokens, so start with a short one.

LemoLab · 6:31

Their showcase film: one character walks through all 98 Best Picture winners, each redrawn in a style that fits the film. Every frame and every note was written in code by Claude.

Two commands in Claude Code.

Then say what you want. This is the README's own example:

Make a 45-second film in the watercolor style about the coffee farm my family runs.
  • You'll need

    Node 20 or newer, ffmpeg 7 or newer, and Python 3.11 or newer (or uv). Claude installs the rest.

  • For a Reel, ask for vertical

    Films come out at 1920 by 1080 by default. Ask for 1080 by 1920 and you get a vertical film.

  • Use Opus 5.5

    The styles are tuned for Claude Opus 5.5. Other models may not reproduce them well.

05Make it yours

Turn any style into your own.

This is the part that matters for a business: a look that stays the same across every film you make.

2

Start from a library style.

Ask Claude to change a library style, or describe a look in your own words, or share a picture or film you like and it picks the closest style as a starting point. Add your brand colours, logo, fonts and recurring characters. Your style is kept in ~/lemo-my-styles, library updates never touch it, and the next film you ask for can use it by name.

06Where to start

Pick your starting point.

Start with one short film in one style, then build from there.

  • A product or launch video

    Try Dark Tech Keynote or Glass Product Render.

  • Numbers you need to explain

    Try Data Storytelling, or one of the Evidence Films, where every number on screen is sourced.

  • Content for your brand

    Pick the library style closest to your look and make it your own first, so every film after that matches.

  • Just to see what it can do

    Watercolor Brush or 16-bit Pixel RPG, about 30 seconds long, vertical.
