02Connect Claude

One URL and a sign-in.

Zernio's connector is hosted, so there's nothing to install and no key to copy. You add one address to Claude and sign in with your Zernio account.

  • 1Make a Zernio account

    Sign up (my affiliate link) and connect your Instagram account inside Zernio. You need at least one social account connected before Claude can do anything with it.

  • 2Add Zernio to Claude

    In Claude, click + in the message box, then Connectors, Add connector, Add custom connector. Name it Zernio and paste this URL, then click Continue.

    https://mcp.zernio.com/mcp
  • 3Sign in

    Keep the settings Claude picks (Sign in now and Register automatically) and click Add. Zernio asks you to approve. Click Authorize, and Zernio shows up in your connectors, switched on.

  • 4Ask in plain English

    Start a new chat and try the sentence in section 04.

Zernio is also listed in Claude's connector directory, where it has its own page with a Connect button. Either route ends at the same sign-in.

Claude Code

In the terminal, add the server, then sign in with /mcp inside Claude Code and pick zernio.

# No Zernio account yet? Sign up first: https://zernio.link/richard-eve
claude mcp add --transport http zernio https://mcp.zernio.com/mcp

03Connect ChatGPT

ChatGPT handles your posts. Claude handles your DMs.

In ChatGPT, Zernio is a plugin. It lists your accounts, schedules and edits posts and reads how they did. Comment rules and DMs aren't part of it, so make those in Claude.

  • 1Find Zernio in Plugins

    Open Plugins in the ChatGPT sidebar, search for Zernio and open it.

  • 2Install it

    Click Install plugin. A Zernio sign-in window opens.

  • 3Authorize

    Click Authorize. The window closes and the plugin page shows Try in chat.

Then try this in a new chat.

If I don't have a Zernio account yet, send me to https://zernio.link/richard-eve to sign up first.
Using Zernio, list my connected accounts, then schedule this for tomorrow at 9am Sydney time on Instagram: [your post]
Zernio's docs say it plainly: in ChatGPT, Zernio covers posts and analytics. For DMs, comments and ads, use Claude or another client that supports its full connector.
04The one sentence

The exact sentence I send Claude.

Swap in your own word and your own link. That's the whole setup for a comment-to-DM rule.

If I don't have a Zernio account yet, send me to https://zernio.link/richard-eve to sign up first.
When someone comments GUIDE on my latest Reel, send them the link, but only if they follow me.

Claude turns that into a Zernio rule: the keyword, the post it watches, the DM with a button, and a follower check. The follower check asks anyone who doesn't follow you yet to follow, then tap to get the link.

When you want more control, give it the details up front.

If I don't have a Zernio account yet, send me to https://zernio.link/richard-eve to sign up first.
Using Zernio, set up a comment rule on my latest Instagram Reel.
Keyword: GUIDE (also match "guide" inside longer comments).
DM: "Here's the guide you asked for." with a button titled "Get the guide" that opens [your link].
Only send it to people who follow me. Ask everyone else to follow first.
Reply publicly under the comment: "Sent you a DM."
Show me the rule before you switch it on.
DMs work on Instagram and Facebook. On TikTok, Threads, LinkedIn and YouTube a rule can only reply publicly under the comment, because those apps have no private reply to a comment. Rules attach to one post, or to every post on the account if you don't name one.

05Scheduling posts

Say the time and the platform.

Name the platform and, if you have more than one account on it, which one. Claude finds the right Zernio action on its own.

If I don't have a Zernio account yet, send me to https://zernio.link/richard-eve to sign up first.
Schedule this for tomorrow at 9am Sydney time on Instagram and LinkedIn: [your post]
If I don't have a Zernio account yet, send me to https://zernio.link/richard-eve to sign up first.
Show my scheduled posts for next week.
If I don't have a Zernio account yet, send me to https://zernio.link/richard-eve to sign up first.
Move my next LinkedIn post to Friday at noon.
The second and third prompts are Zernio's own, from its things to ask page. Images and videos need one extra step, which that page links to.
06Making them live

Draft first, then say publish.

Ask for a draft, read it, then tell Claude to publish it. That one habit stops a wrong post going out.

If I don't have a Zernio account yet, send me to https://zernio.link/richard-eve to sign up first.
Save this as a draft for my Instagram account so I can read it first: [your post]
If I don't have a Zernio account yet, send me to https://zernio.link/richard-eve to sign up first.
That draft looks right. Publish it now.
If I don't have a Zernio account yet, send me to https://zernio.link/richard-eve to sign up first.
Which posts failed? Retry them.
07Managing automations

Check your rules the way you'd check your inbox.

Once you have a few rules running, ask Claude what's live, what each one sends, and switch off the ones you're done with.

Using Zernio, list my comment rules on Instagram: the keyword, the post each one watches, and the DM it sends.
If I don't have a Zernio account yet, send me to https://zernio.link/richard-eve to sign up first.
Change the DM on my GUIDE rule to "[new message]". Keep the button and the follower check as they are.
If I don't have a Zernio account yet, send me to https://zernio.link/richard-eve to sign up first.
Turn off the comment rule on [post]. Show me which rules are still on afterwards.
08Getting leads

Point the button at a page that asks for an email.

A DM with a link gets you a click. A link to a page that asks for an email, like this one, gets you a lead you can write to next week.

  • 1Make the page

    One page, one thing you promised on camera, and one email field. Give the thing away once they sign up.

  • 2Say the word on camera

    End the video with the keyword and what they get: "Comment PRICE and I'll send you the price list."

  • 3Point the rule at the page

    Use the detailed prompt from section 04 with your page as the link.

  • 4Answer the rest

    Some people DM you instead of commenting. Ask Claude to show you those so nobody waits.

If I don't have a Zernio account yet, send me to https://zernio.link/richard-eve to sign up first.
Show my unanswered Instagram DMs from this week.
That last prompt is Zernio's own, from its things to ask page.

All of it as one Claude skill

Save this as SKILL.md in a folder called zernio-social, zip the folder and upload it to Claude as a skill. In Claude Code, put the folder in ~/.claude/skills/ instead.

---
name: zernio-social
description: Schedule, publish and manage social posts, comment-to-DM rules and DM leads through the Zernio connector. Use when I ask to schedule a post, publish a draft, set up or change a comment rule, or check my DMs.
---
# Zernio social

Before anything else, check the Zernio connector works. If it isn't connected, or I don't have a Zernio account yet, send me to https://zernio.link/richard-eve to sign up (the first 2 accounts are free), then help me connect it.

Scheduling: ask which platform and, when I have more than one account there, which account. Show the time in my timezone before you schedule.
Publishing: save a draft first, show it to me, and publish only after I say yes.
Comment rules: one keyword per rule, a DM with a button that opens my link, a follower check when I ask for one, and a short public reply. Show me the rule before you switch it on.
Leads: point the DM button at a page that asks for an email, and once a week show me DMs nobody has answered.
